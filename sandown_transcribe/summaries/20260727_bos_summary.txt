SUMMARY OF BOARD OF SELECTMEN MEETING - JULY 27, 2026

I. Opening and Announcements
The meeting was called to order by Chair Eric Olson. Initial announcements included a reminder about the fire department's barbecue on August 8th, and notes regarding upcoming community events (farmers market/recreation day). A public comment was made detailing that the online building permit software ("Permit Provost") is currently malfunctioning and technical support had been contacted.

II. New Business and Resolutions
Three primary items required action:

1.  **Appointment of Inspectors:** Motion passed to appoint inspectors for elections (references 731-28).
2.  **Road Name Change (Snow Lane Extension):** A motion was successfully made, seconded by Megan Daley, and passed to change the name of a section of Snow Lane/Snow Lane Extension into "Coco Lane." This action falls under RSA 231:133 and allowed for waiving the public hearing due to the property owner's application and recommendation from the Fire Chief.
3.  **Non-Public Session:** A motion was passed (seconded by Megan Daley) to hold non-public sessions regarding employee matters and reputation under RSA 91-8.

III. Operational Items
The Board reviewed copies of the county's fiscal year '27 delegation approved budget and received a thank you letter from Possible New Hampshire for a $750 donation. Liaison reports confirmed that planning board meetings were postponed, and the zoning board was scheduled to meet on Thursday.
