2027 Budget wage guidance
- Discussion of non-union wage increases for FY2027. Guidance requested for department budgets.
- Gail provided cost estimates for matrix pay increases, excluding union contract employees.
- 3% matrix step increase = $33,666.
- Each additional 1% ≈ $19,109.
- CPI currently ~3.5-3.8%.
- Discussion of retention, turnover costs, default budget risk, tax rate impact from school increase, and legislative changes to default budget process.
- Board consensus to provide guideline of 3% matrix step increase plus 1% COLA = 4% total, approx $52,775.
- Motion to set 2027 wage guideline at 3% matrix step increase + 1% COLA for non-union staff. Second by Tom. Passed 3-1.
