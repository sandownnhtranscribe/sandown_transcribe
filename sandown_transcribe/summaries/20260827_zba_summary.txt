Sandown Zoning Board of Adjustment – 27 Aug 2026, 6:30 pm

Chairman: Jason Hue. Voting members present: Dan Hue – alternate voting tonight, Dave Adolino, John White, Mike Bergeron. Building inspector recuses from the Marrows hearing.

Minutes
- June 25, 2026 minutes accepted as amended. Motion passed 3-1.
- July 30, 2026 minutes accepted as written. One abstention.

Application deferred – Brady & Olivia Peterson
- 41 North Shore Road, map 29 lot 51. Variance request from Art 2 Part A Sec 13B and Art 3 Part A Sec 1A for non-conforming use and 30-ft setback from public right-of-way. Proposed 16x30 ft addition to existing non-conforming dwelling.
- Applicants not present. Board moves public hearing up and holds the application.

Public hearing – Mark & Jennifer Marrows – variance granted
- 18 Brian Street, map 21 lot 41. Variance from Art 2 Part B Sec 3B for frontage building requirement: minimum 200 ft, lot has 175 ft.
- Building inspector recuses, moves to audience. Applicants present.
- Applicants: lot is 175 ft frontage on a Class 5 town road, ample space for safe driveway and sight lines, all other zoning requirements met – setbacks, septic, well.
- Board discussion on Sec 13A non-conforming lot exception vs variance. Board clarifies Sec 13A still requires sufficient frontage, which is defined as 200 ft, so variance is needed.
- Letter from abutter read into record raising 7-8 points on legal basis, hardship, precedent, drainage, etc. Board addresses each:
  1. Non-conforming lot status – Sec 13A does not waive the 200 ft requirement.
  2. Historical legal status – deed of record 1976 and 2024 is sufficient; title search would have cleared issues.
  3. Hardship – lack of 200 ft frontage is the special condition; no lot line adjustment possible.
  4. Reasonable use – board is limited to frontage issue, not broader site design.
  5. Purpose of 200 ft – to avoid overcrowding; driveway permit will address sight lines.
  6. Precedent – zoning has no precedent.
  7. Drainage/grading – beyond ZBA scope, building inspector issue.
- No public comment. Motion to grant variance from Art 2 Part B Sec 3B for 18 Brian Street to permit single-family home with 175 ft frontage. Seconded, unanimous aye. Board notes 30-day appeal period and findings: no unnecessary hardship, no safety issue, lot size comparable to neighbors.

Peterson application – completeness review
- Board returns to 41 North Shore Road. Abutters checked, application deemed complete.
- Plans reviewed: existing house with proposed addition, quarter-inch scale. Current setback ~10 ft from street, neighborhood is tight.
- Motion to accept application for variance from Art 2 Part A Sec 13B and Art 3 Part A Sec 1A for 16x30 ft addition to non-conforming dwelling. Seconded, approved with one abstention. Application scheduled for next month's agenda.
