**Sandown Zoning Board of Adjustments Meeting Summary**
**Date:** September 24, 2026

**I. Minutes Acceptance**
The motion to accept the minutes from the August 27, 2026, zoning board meeting as written was made and seconded, passing unanimously.

**II. Public Hearing Items & Variances**
1.  **Zoning Variance Permit:** A proposal for a zone ordinance variance permit (for a 16x30 addition to an existing nonconforming dwelling) at 41 North Shore Road, Santown, NH (Map 29 lot 51), was reviewed. The applicant requested that the board continue consideration until the October 29th meeting.
2.  **New Board Member Discussion:** A volunteer expressed interest in joining the Zoning Board as an alternate member. Board members strongly emphasized adherence to procedural rules, including maintaining absolute impartiality and discretion when discussing matters outside of formal sessions (e.g., avoiding public comments on social media). It was confirmed that board appointments fall under the authority of the Selectmen, not the Board itself.

**III. Financial & Policy Discussions**
1.  **Advertising/Application Fees:** Extensive discussion took place regarding the inadequacy of the current $125 fee for newspaper ads and general application fees. The cost range reviewed ranged from $103 to $343 per article, making the existing fee inaccurate.
    *   **Motion Passed:** A motion was passed to raise the fee for newspaper notifications listed on the ZBA application form from $125 to $200.
    *   **Application Correction:** A second motion was passed to update and correct the current application form (under Section X D, Part 2) to accurately reflect a mandatory fee of five dollars plus postage costs.

**IV. Budget Review & Adjustments**
The board reviewed and approved several line-item budget adjustments for the next fiscal period:
*   **Payroll:** Increased due to a 9% COLA adjustment ($1,606).
*   **Legal:** Kept level funded at $1,000.
*   **Seminars:** Set at $180.
*   **Advertising Fees:** Adjusted to $1,500 (reflecting the increased application fee structure).
*   **Supplies/Books:** Maintained at $50 and $75, respectively.

The board adjourned after passing these resolutions.
