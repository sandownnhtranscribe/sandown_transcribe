IV. ENVIRONMENTAL AND WATER QUALITY CONCERNS (Key Focus Area)
A. Testing Frequency & Scope: Commissioners expressed deep concern regarding the current biennial water testing schedule (next test due 2028). The discussion highlighted the need for more proactive and frequent monitoring, given increased development in the area.
B. Contaminants of Concern: It was stressed that water testing must be expanded beyond standard procedures to include contaminants like PFAS (Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances), which were noted as being present despite previous assurances.
C. Cumulative Impact: Commissioners emphasized that current assessments do not account for the cumulative impact of multiple developments across connected local waterways, suggesting a need for stronger review protocols.
D. Public Advisory Recommendation: Due to the nature of private well ownership in the area, it was recommended that the Commission draft and distribute a Public Service Announcement (PSA) advising all residents to test their private wells regularly regardless of mandated state testing schedules.

V. HISTORICAL AND ARTIFACT FINDINGS (Metal Detecting Discussion)
A. Concern: Concerns were raised regarding unmonitored ground disturbance in public lands, particularly potential discoveries of historical or archaeological artifacts (Native American materials, 1800s coins, weapons/ammunition).
B. Mitigation: It was emphasized that any discovered items must be documented and reported to the Commission for historical evaluation, ensuring compliance with federal guidelines (e.g., NAGPRA related concerns).

VI. ACTIONS AND NEXT STEPS
A. Water Testing PSA Draft: A member agreed to draft a Public Service Announcement (PSA) regarding private well water testing, to be presented at the next meeting.
B. Research Follow-up: Scott Potter will follow up on environmental studies and potentially develop a recommendation for stronger development review protocols based on current findings.
C. Metal Detecting Liaison: Tim Robinson will follow up with local historical experts (Rob Morris in Hampstead) regarding best practices for permitting metal detecting activities to ensure archaeological sensitivity.
