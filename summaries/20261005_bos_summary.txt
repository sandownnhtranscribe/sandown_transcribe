Summary of Board of Selectmen Meeting - October 5, 2026

This meeting covered the approval of previous minutes, public feedback, major capital expenditures, and budget approvals for 2027.

I. Administrative Items:
- Minutes Approval: The board approved the public and non-public minutes from September 28, 2026.
- Zoning Board Appointment: Diane Romnick was appointed as a three-year Alternate Member of the Zoning Board.

II. Public Comment & Community Updates:
- Election Signage: A resident stressed the need for better town signage to educate the public about non-November elections (e.g., March primaries).
- Oktoberfest: The committee provided details on upcoming activities, including dates and vendors.
- Historical Society: Updates were given regarding the effort to restore the Train Depot Museum. An initial building assessment plan was approved, aiming for a matching grant from external preservation groups.

III. 2027 Budget Review & Approvals:
The board reviewed and approved budgets across numerous departments, including Code Enforcement ($4,847), Health Officer ($5,817), Building Inspection ($52,475 gross), Plumbing ($43,000 gross), Septic Inspector ($10,120 gross), etc. Notable approvals included:
- A funding increase for Patriotic Purposes ($7,350) to incorporate fireworks costs.

IV. Capital Improvement & Development Planning:
- **Roads:** Discussions centered on paving and the need for a comprehensive survey to manage town growth and infrastructure decay (e.g., hot box repairs).
- **Future Planning:** The board acknowledged the necessity of future planning for expansion and facility upgrades, with multiple parties agreeing that a detailed site survey is required.

V. New Business:
- **Mailing Machine Lease:** Approved renewal of the lease agreement for the folding/mail machine at a discounted rate ($166.24/month).
- **Donation Acceptance:** Accepted a donation to the Senior Citizens Transportation Fund.
- **Zoning Board Alternate:** Motion passed to appoint Diane Romnick as an alternate member (staggered term).

The meeting concluded with discussions on future planning, including potential funding streams and required certifications for large municipal projects.
