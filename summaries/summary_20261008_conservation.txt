## Sandown Conservation Commission Meeting Summary (October 8, 2026)

**Source File:** 20261008-conservation.log
**Summary Date:** [Current Date]

### I. Environmental Concerns & Water Quality (DES Report)
*   **Water Sampling:** A request was reviewed for water sampling of properties on Wells Village Road due to blasting by Vantage Point in the area. The Commission gave approval for this monitoring to proceed, which is being conducted by Edgewater Strategies.
*   **Impaired Waters List:** Discussion focused heavily on the New Hampshire DES draft list of impaired surface waters, which requires comments by October 16th. Key local bodies flagged include **Phillips Pond**, **Lily Pond**, and **Shoal Pond**. The report identifies concerns regarding bacteria (E. coli) and pH levels in these areas.
*   **Action Item:** It was emphasized that the Commission needs a concerted effort to educate the public on wetland protection, as many residents are unaware of regulations (e.g., setbacks, grant/waiver options). A plan for recurring wetlands education workshops is proposed for 2027.

### II. Financial Updates
*   **Land Use Change Tax Account:** To maximize returns and maintain liquidity, funds were reallocated: $250,000 was moved into a higher interest liquid account (approx. 3%), and an additional $750,000 is placed in a 2-month CD bearing 3.5%.
*   **Education & Awareness Budget:** The budget for educational programs must be reviewed to ensure proper accounting of supplies, postage, and materials used (e.g., signage, flyers). Future funds should account for multiple educational initiatives.
*   **Office Supplies/Fees:** Discussion confirmed that necessary general office supplies (folders, minutes) may need to be allocated through a dedicated "office supplies" line item rather than absorbed by other accounts like 'Education and Awareness'.

### III. Infrastructure & Maintenance Projects
*   **Trail Markers and Signage:** It was decided that the Commission should plan a bulk order for U-channel T posts (steel, coated) for various locations including Wells Village Road, Fremont Road/Town Forest, and Odell Road to prevent multiple shipments charges. The cost for four posts is $140.
*   **Gate Locks:** Action item: Follow up with the Fire Chief regarding the availability of three additional locks for other gate locations.
*   **Red Barn Maintenance:** Noted that items related to Spooky Walk and Oktoberfest are being cleared from the second floor and stored at the Ed Garvey Center.

### IV. Meeting Logistics & Future Planning
*   **Meeting Minutes Acceptance:** The minutes from 9/24/26 were accepted as written.
*   **Future Focus Areas:** Key recurring topics identified for future action include: comprehensive wetlands education, securing sufficient funds for educational outreach, and planning the next trail maintenance work committee after foliage season.

***
**Key Decisions & Action Items:**
1.  Review and comment on the DES impaired surface waters report before October 16th (related to local ponds).
2.  Coordinate a bulk purchase of U-channel posts for all required signage locations.
