SUMMARY OF SANDOWN PLANNING BOARD MEETING MINUTES - OCTOBER 20, 2026
---

**Overview:** The meeting reviewed multiple site plan applications and zoning variances (KRKR Trust, NTV Builders LLC, AM Lake Family Trust, Sanctuary Estates LLC), all of which were continued to November 17th. Board business included approving the 2027 Cleaning Board Budget.

**I. KEY APPLICATIONS & FINDINGS:**
*   **Sanctuary Estates LLC (Camping Cabins):** The most detailed review covered extensive topics including environmental impact, noise modeling, and water/septic approvals from DES. Key public concerns repeatedly raised were:
    *   **Setbacks:** Violation of 75-foot residential setbacks with plans showing only a 20-foot setback.
    *   **Dumpster Placement:** Zoning mandates require dumpsters to be located *behind* a building, which was observed on the plan.
    *   **Operational Plan:** A major focus was on making all operational rules (e.g., quiet hours, trash handling, limited occupancy) legally binding, definitive conditions of approval, moving away from "suggestions."

*   **Zoning/Variance Issues:** Multiple hearings involved granting waivers for zoning requirements (e.g., single access street length, high intensity soil mapping).

**II. BOARD BUSINESS:**
- **Budget Approval:** The 2027 Cleaning Board Budget was approved with net expenses totaling $16,967.

**III. OVERALL TAKEAWAY:**
The central thread throughout the proceedings was the consensus that proposed developments must demonstrate adherence to strict zoning regulations and incorporate robust, definitive operational plans and enforceable conditions to protect existing residents and maintain town character. The need for "definitive" terms over mere suggestions was a recurring theme among board members and public commentators.
