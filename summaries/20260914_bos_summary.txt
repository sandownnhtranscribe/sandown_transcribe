Board Meeting Summary - September 14, 2026
File Name: summaries/20260914_bos_summary.txt

The meeting commenced with the Pledge of Allegiance and reviewed the minutes from August 31st (both public and non-public records were accepted by motion).

Key Discussion Points & Actions:

1. Infrastructure & Development Concerns (Public Comment): A concerned taxpayer addressed the board regarding significant population influx to Sandown, noting that this growth is outpacing local infrastructure planning. He highlighted concerns over school additions, fire/sprinkler systems, and general municipal capacity.
2. Forestry Vehicle Bid: The Board reviewed a bid for an excess military vehicle (a 2002 Stewart Stevenson's cab-over deuce and a half) from the Town of Newington. Discussion centered on its robustness compared to modern vehicles and significant cost savings ($140k estimated build vs $200k+ replacement). The board confirmed the bidding procedure required is not an auction, but a sealed bid process.
3. Hazardous Mitigation Plan Grant: The Board reviewed grant funding from the Rockingham Planning Commission for updating the town’s hazardous mitigation plan (due to expire Sept 30th). After review of the terms and scope, the board motioned, and subsequently passed, the acceptance of the grant agreement. This funding is structured to allow payments via time/labor rather than cash.
4. Fuel Bids: The Board reviewed bids for oil, gas, diesel, and propane as of September 30th. Due to pricing volatility (terminal pricing), the board motioned and passed entering a one-year contract with:
    *   Palmer Gas: Oil at $389.90/gallon; Propane at $1.99/gallon.
    *   Half Nurse: Gas/Diesel over Irving Terminal pricing ($0.499/gallon fixed rate).
5. Community & Miscellaneous Updates:
    *   The Oktoberfest Committee updated the public on raffle ticket sales, vendors (e.g., cornhole boards, grain sack toss), and planned entertainment for October 17th, emphasizing volunteer needs.
    *   The Library Committee requested permission and volunteers for a beautification project at the library's overgrown property this coming Saturday.
    *   Mosquito reports confirmed continuous monitoring until the first frost; no spraying was required.

Next Meeting: The next scheduled board meeting is set for September 28th.
