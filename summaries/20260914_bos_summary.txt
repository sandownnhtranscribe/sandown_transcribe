II. PUBLIC COMMENTARY & AGENDA ITEMS
The floor was opened for Public Comment:

A. Oktoberfest Committee Update (Karen Jortberg/Erica Olsen):
*   **Purpose:** To inform the public about the ongoing Octoberfest event scheduled for October 17th.
*   **Fundraising:** They are still selling raffle tickets for cornhole boards ($5 per piece or five for $20). All funds are raised through donations.
*   **Activities/Vendors:** The event will feature activities including grain sack toss, music (The Stumpy Joe Band and Overlander Off Brew Band), beer, food (including German and Italian cuisine), and a bonfire.
*   **Call to Action:** Volunteers are needed for setup and teardown. Payment can be made via Venmo, requiring the "paper trail" of town funds.

B. Concerns Regarding Growth & Infrastructure (Tom Tamburello):
*   Mr. Tamburello expressed significant concern regarding the influx of new residents to Sandown from out-of-state (mostly Massachusetts).
*   He plans to meet with the Superintendent of Schools and the Fire Chief to discuss the potential strain on town infrastructure, including:
    *   The school needing additions.
    *   Fire/sprinkler system upgrades.

V. VEHICLE BIDS AND PLANNING BOARD UPDATES (Contextual):
The meeting also included extensive discussion on large-scale development and equipment:
*   **NOAA Estates Development:** Presented by Ryan Lavelle (NTV Builders, LLC), this project proposes developing a 62.75-acre property at 112 Little Mill Road into NOAA Estates (21 single-family dwelling units). The plan is described as an open space subdivision using zero-lot line condominium ownership, with individual wells and septic systems for each home.
*   **Next Steps:** NTV Builders highlighted substantial progress in the drainage report and supporting materials revision after incorporating town and AOT comments. Remaining steps include final sign-off from engineering review, state subdivision approval, alteration of terrain, and Conservation Commission process completion. The committee advised following up on condo documents with Diane for formal review.
*   **Fire Apparatus Bid:** Mike Divine presented an opportunity to bid on a retired fire vehicle (LMTV Light Tactical Vehicle) being sold by the Town of Newington. The board considered placing a bid amount of $12,500.
