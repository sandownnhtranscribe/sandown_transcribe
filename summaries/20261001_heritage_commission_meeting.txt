SANDOWN HERITAGE COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES - OCTOBER 1, 2026

I. Meeting Administration & Finance
*   Minutes from the September 3rd meeting were accepted as written.
*   Donations/Expenditures: $212.12 profit was generated from t-shirt sales. T-shirts and tumblers remain available at Town Hall or the Commission's house.

II. Projects, Events & Announcements
*   Cancelled Activities: The Heritage Fair and the planned "review of the Big House / Little House" presentation were cancelled due to lack of participation/vendors.
*   Upcoming Events Highlighted:
    1.  **Chester Guided Tour:** Saturday, 9 a.m. (Focuses on Sandown residents and French family history).
    2.  **Peter Wigan Presentation:** Topic on the Norfolk Line and Wheelwright Deed. Date: Saturday, October 17th at 1 p.m., at America's Stonehenge. The line is historically significant as it settled colonial conflicts between different groups.
    3.  **Depot Paranormal Event:** October 24th, 7-9 pm.
    4.  **NIRA Fall Conference:** November 13th - November 15th in North Kingstown, RI (for general archaeological interest).
*   Annual Report: The report is due the first couple of weeks of January. Members are encouraged to submit any notes or articles for inclusion.

III. Site Updates & Preservation Efforts
*   Red Barn Update: Commission members cleaned out a cluttered stall and swept the barn. The facility is being restored, with future improvements planned for broken windows (front/back).
*   Archaeological Findings (Private Chamber): Discussion centered on a potentially significant pre-colonial structure behind Perinos property. Due to current development and blasting activity, non-invasive investigation via core drilling or remote drone camera was suggested over physical removal. Volunteers are needed as the state is unsupportive due to funding issues.
*   Research Focus: The Commission emphasized building up resources on the website with academic articles (Native American settlements, smallpox history) rather than relying solely on written submissions from members.

IV. Next Steps & Logistics
*   Next Meeting Dates: Conservation commission meets on the 1st and 3rd Thursday of November and December; Heritage Commission meets on the second Thursday (November 12th). The next formal meeting is scheduled for November 12th.
