Summary of Sandown Planning Board Meeting - July 21, 2026
==============================================================

**I. Minutes Approval (Administrative Items)**
The board approved the minutes for previous meetings:
1. June 16th meeting minutes were approved as written.
2. Site walk minutes (July 7, 2026) were approved as written.

**II. Public Hearing: DJV Properties, LLC - 327 Main Street**
A continued public hearing was held for an application seeking to change the use and amend the site plan at 327 Main Street, involving a proposed commercial building (2,745 sq ft) and parking reconfiguration for an attached ADU.

*   **Developer Presentation:** The applicant presented revised plans addressing previous concerns. Key submissions included high-intensity soil survey mapping, test-fit information, lot coverage calculations, detailed landscape plan (Sheet 5), and erosion/sedimentation control plan (Sheet 6).
*   **Key Technical Findings:**
    *   Sewage Disposal: Soil mapping confirms 1,458 gallons per day of on-site capacity, with the proposed use generating 1,330 GPD, leaving a remaining buffer of 128 GPD.
    *   Stormwater: The developer confirmed that storm events volume discharged to Main Street is reduced compared to current conditions.
*   **Outcome:** After discussion regarding necessary state permits (NH DOT driveways for residential and commercial use; water/septic), the board voted to grant **conditional approval**.
*   **Conditions Set by Board:** Approval is contingent upon receiving five items:
    1. Amended DOT driveway permit for modified existing commercial site driveway at 323 Main Street.
    2. DOT driveway permit for existing residents at 327 Main Street.
    3. NHDES water supply approval.
    4. NHDES construction approval for septic system.
    5. Owner posting a performance guarantee to cover erosion control and site stabilization costs.

**III. Other Business & Administrative Updates**
*   The board reviewed and approved multiple administrative items, including bond pieces (e.g., Mylaws at 48 Hurst Road) and the release of funds from two PREA accounts (H141 and R193 transmission lines for Eversource Energy), as work was confirmed complete.
*   Discussions included updates on local development projects, such as the ongoing construction at Vantage Point and Hersey Highlands, noting continued activity in improving local infrastructure.

**IV. Next Steps & Future Planning**
The board noted several items requiring future review:
*   Fee Schedule Review: A discussion was held regarding whether the current municipal permit fees need to be revisited due to inflation and cost of living increases over the last five years.
*   Open Space Development: The condo association (Angle Pond) was advised that any amendments to their bylaws require reviewing compliance with zoning ordinances, specifically concerning open space management.
The meeting adjourned following unanimous consent.
