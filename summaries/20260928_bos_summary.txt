Board of Selectmen Meeting Summary: September 28, 2026

The Board called to order for the meeting on September 28, 2026. Following the Pledge of Allegiance, the board proceeded with standard business items.

I. Minutes Approval
Minutes from the previous meeting (September 14th, 2026) were reviewed and formally approved by a motion.

II. Public Comment
Public comment was dominated by promotional announcements for the upcoming Octoberfest event on October 17th at Sandlots. Highlights included:
*   The availability of German food, beer, and polka music.
*   A raffle of cornhole boards with American flags ($5 tickets). Bulk discounts (5 tickets for $20) were encouraged.

III. New Business
Two donations were approved by motion:
1.  Senior Transportation Donation Fund.
2.  Old Home Day Donation Fund (a donation check for $30).

IV. Old Business & Updates
Discussion was held regarding the newly launched website, noting that department liaisons need to ensure all links are functioning and directing users correctly across different sections of the site.

V. Conclusion and Next Steps
The meeting entered a non-public session under RSA 91-A:3 (Roman Numeral II e) for litigation and employee matters, specifically discussing Police Union negotiations related to collective bargaining, which were noted as exempt from public record requirements. The board adjourned with the next meeting scheduled for October 5th, 2026.
