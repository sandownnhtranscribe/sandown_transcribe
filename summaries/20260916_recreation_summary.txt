Summary of Sandown Recreation Commission Meeting - September 16, 2026

I. Minutes Approval
The motion was made and seconded to approve the minutes from September 2nd, 2026, with an amendment made regarding a typo noted on the last page, line 7. The amended minutes were approved.

II. Financial Review & Budget Updates
A. RecDesk Payment: Funds previously allocated to "programs" for RecDesk payments were moved to "technology and internet," although the total amount remained unchanged; only the line item was adjusted.
B. Critical Deficits/Updates:
*   **Mowing:** The mowing budget is currently zero, as bids are being collected. A new budget must account for this expense when funds become available.
*   **Personnel Costs:** There was a discussion about increases in staffing costs (e.g., lifeguard payroll) due to changes in employment status (full-time vs. part-time).
*   **Future Figures:** The most updated financial numbers are not yet available, as the town's finance director was on vacation and summer camp figures will not be processed until October.
C. Budget Strategy: The committee noted that the current budget is stable but emphasized that the final full-year budget must incorporate the expected increases in mowing costs and potential staff changes.

III. Infrastructure & Facility Planning (Old Business)
A. Ice Rink Feasibility: Discussion resumed regarding hosting hockey vs. general skating. PrimeX confirmed that designated time slots are possible for both activities, but they cannot run simultaneously. The committee needs adequate lighting to support evening hours when the sun sets. A significant point was raised about needing a potential generator light system or limiting usage during dark months/winter.
B. Pavilion & Playground Equipment: There is interest in purchasing playground equipment and potentially improving facilities. Given concerns over costs, the committee explored options like renting temporary structures (e.g., 30x60 ft pavilion) to mitigate immediate capital expenditure risk.

IV. Community Events Planning
A. Spooky Walk: The commission plans to revive a "Spooky Walk" event for next year. Initial proposals include sectioning off areas in the parking lot or using available structures (like the Red Barn inventory).
B. Coordination: To maximize attendance and interest, it was suggested that the Spooky Walk be scheduled concurrently with the annual Monster Mash festival on October 23rd.

V. Next Meeting Logistics
The next meeting date was set for September 30th. The chair confirmed this date is possible and will circulate a location/details shortly after coordinating quorum confirmations.
